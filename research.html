Time-Resolving Supermassive Black Holes from Low-Earth Orbit

A research note on instrumentation for time-resolved X-ray observation of supermassive black holes from a CubeSat-class platform. Examines the trade-space between aperture, photon-counting cadence, and onboard data reduction, and proposes a pipeline tuned for variability studies under tight telemetry budgets.

Comparing Transformer Neural Architecture to Recurrent Neural Networks for Natural Language Understanding

A systematic comparison of transformer- and RNN-based architectures on common NLU benchmarks: classification accuracy, inference cost, and scaling behaviour under limited data. Isolates where attention provides genuine inductive bias and where its gains collapse back to better-tuned recurrence.

Optimum Tilt Angle for Photovoltaic Solar Panels

A derivation of the optimal fixed tilt angle for photovoltaic panels at a given latitude, modelled as a calculus optimisation problem against annual solar irradiance. Compares the closed-form result against empirical solar position data and quantifies the energy penalty of common installer rules of thumb.

Effect of Mordants on Percentage Absorption of Rubia Cardifolia Dye

An experimental study measuring how different metal-salt mordants modulate the spectral absorption of Rubia cardifolia dye on cotton substrates. UV-Vis spectroscopy compares absorption efficiency across mordant species and concentrations, with a discussion of the underlying complexation chemistry.
